Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

West Product Development LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website westproductdev.com (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit the Site, how it is used, and what choices you have under California and U.S. law.

Information we collect

The Site is a static marketing website. We do not run analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising tags, session-replay tools, chat widgets, or any other technology that records visitor behavior. The Site does not include forms, sign-ups, accounts, or e-commerce.

The only information collected when you visit is the standard operational data that any web server records:

  • Your IP address
  • The pages you request
  • Your browser type and operating system
  • The date and time of your visit

This data is collected by our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) for security, performance monitoring, and abuse prevention. We do not use it for marketing or share it with third parties for marketing.

Third parties

Two third parties are involved in delivering the Site to you:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the Site. Vercel’s privacy practices are described at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • jsDelivr (a content delivery network) serves the Geist font stylesheet used by the Site. jsDelivr’s privacy practices are described at jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net.

We do not provide either service with information identifying you as an individual. We do not control what those services collect through their own operation.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies. Vercel may set strictly technical cookies for security and load balancing; these are not used for tracking or marketing.

How we use information

We use the operational data described above only to:

  • Operate, monitor, and secure the Site
  • Respond to abuse, technical issues, or legal requests

We do not sell, share, or rent any visitor data to third parties for any purpose.

Your California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at westproductdev@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell or share personal information for any purpose, including for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is no opt-out mechanism needed because we have nothing to opt out of. If California law later requires further action, we will update this section.

Children

The Site is intended for adult business users. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Updates
