Method

How it runs

1

Interview everyone

CEO down to the coordinators. Sales, design, project management, construction, service, procurement, finance, admin, IT. Each person walks through their actual day and names what slows them down.

2

Map the stack and the handoffs

Every tool in use, what it costs, who touches it, and what happens in the gaps between departments. The breakages are almost never inside a department. They’re at the seams.

3

Rank it, then build

Each fix gets scoped, priced, and sorted by whether it grows revenue or cuts cost. Then the build starts, connecting what already exists or writing new software where nothing fits.

Findings

The kind of thing it turns up

Paying twice

A capacity planning tool already included in a subscription they were paying for, unused, while the same job was being done by hand in a spreadsheet.

Orphaned handoff

The handoff from construction to service had no owner. Everyone assumed the other department picked it up.

Re-entry

The same job data typed into three systems by three different people, because none of the systems talk to each other.

Overlap

Two paid tools doing substantially the same job, kept because different departments adopted them separately and nobody compared them.
